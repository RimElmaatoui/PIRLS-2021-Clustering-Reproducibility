# Data availability

The analyses use the PIRLS 2021 International Database, Edition 2, published by the International Association for the Evaluation of Educational Achievement (IEA) and the TIMSS & PIRLS International Study Center.

- Official PIRLS 2021 repository: <https://www.iea.nl/data-tools/repository-direct/1970?cycle=1036>
- Dataset DOI: <https://doi.org/10.58150/PIRLS_2021_edition_2_including_Log-file_data>
- IEA disclaimer and license agreement: <https://www.iea.nl/sites/default/files/data-repository/Disclaimer_and_License_Agreement.pdf>

The database is publicly accessible from the official repository, but access and use remain subject to the IEA terms and conditions. For that reason, this code repository does not create a second distribution point for the raw files. Each user should obtain the data directly from IEA and accept the applicable conditions at the authoritative source.

The exact filenames required by the supplied analyses are listed in [`data/README.md`](data/README.md). No raw PIRLS records are included in this repository.

The repository includes aggregate analysis results that do not expose student-level records. It excludes raw questionnaires, individual identifiers, completed feature matrices, cluster assignments, and latent coordinates.

Suggested dataset citation:

> IEA and TIMSS & PIRLS International Study Center. *PIRLS 2021 International Database: Progress in International Reading Literacy Study 2021, Edition 2*. <https://doi.org/10.58150/PIRLS_2021_edition_2_including_Log-file_data>.
